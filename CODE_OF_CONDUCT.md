# Code of conduct

Hejnal is maintained by one person, with help from whoever contributes. Treat
everyone in its issues, pull requests and discussions the way you would want
to be treated at work.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree about code or findings.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or sexual content.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as their address or employer,
  or details of systems they did not choose to share.
- Sustained disruption, such as trolling or spamming threads.

## Reporting

Email [hejnal@legatia.solutions](mailto:hejnal@legatia.solutions). Reports are
read by the maintainer only and kept confidential.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block
people from the repository. Serious or repeated cases lead to a permanent
block.

This applies in the repository and wherever someone represents the project.
